Our Story

The Poké Box was founded from a genuine urge to share a meal that is guilt-free and exciting to eat. We believe you should always look forward to a meal and feel great about what you eat.

The Poké Box began serving delicious poké in Toronto in 2016. Since then we have established our poké as more than a trend, for our customers it has become a lifestyle food.

We are committed to using ingredients that are superior in freshness, flavor, and above all, nutrition. We pick and choose our ingredients carefully, working closely with our purveyors on a daily basis to procure only the best.

The Poke Box prepares our ingredients on a daily basis, crafts our marinades in small batches and makes each meal to order. The result is an exceptional box, filled with delicious proteins, refreshing vegetables, complex sauces, and unique toppings.

Indulge in one of our boxes today and say hello to your new routine! We hope you enjoy our creations as much as we do.

Kevin & Jeremy

Fresh

Our first and foremost mission is to ensure our dinners get the freshest ingredients. To support this mission, our proteins and produce are purchased several times a week. The fish we offer is shipped to us everyday and our menu specials will change with the seasons.

Unique

We offer a changing selection of seasonal ingredients that stem from Hawaii, Japan and Canada. Each ingredient is tried and true before being added to the lineup, we seek to provide something for every individual palette.

Responsible

We believe in sustainability and environmental responsibility. We think our customers will be better able to enjoy our product knowing the story behind each of our ingredients. Our supply and pricing of tuna reflect the constraints of our eco-system.
